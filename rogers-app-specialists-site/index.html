Services

Apps for the job, and a front office while you work.

Android and Google Play only. Custom apps are built, tested and delivered shop-ready. The front office covers the call you cannot pick up.

Custom Android apps

Tell us the job. We build the Android app, test it with real people through Google Play closed testing, and hand you a shop-ready app plus the source code.

  • Built for your brief, for small businesses, tradies, clubs and founders
  • Closed test with 12 or more testers for 14 days, and up to 3 updates from their feedback
  • Store listing, screenshots and a privacy policy on Standard and Pro
  • You get the source files. The app is yours

AI front office

You are on the tools. The phone rings out. The front office texts back, follows up the quote, books the job and asks for the review, so the lead stays yours.

  • Missed-call text-back within a minute
  • Quote follow-ups on day 2 and day 7, then it stops
  • Booking from your real calendar
  • A review request after the job, with no incentive
  • One owner view of every enquiry, plus a summary text at 6pm
  • A 24/7 AI receptionist on the Pro plan

What the front office does with a missed call

  1. 1

    Missed call

    You are on the tools. The phone rings out.

  2. 2

    Instant text

    Within a minute: “Sorry we missed you, what’s the job?”

  3. 3

    Quote follow-up

    A polite nudge on day 2 and day 7, in your business name, with a reply-STOP opt-out.

  4. 4

    Booked and reviewed

    They pick a time from your calendar. After the job, it asks for a Google review.

Your customers’ data stays yours.

If you use the AI front office, we handle the enquiry so we can text back, follow up a quote, book a time and ask for a review. That means a customer’s name, phone number, message and job details.

Customer data is stored with Twilio and may be held overseas. You own that customer data. Ask and we will export it. When a client cancels, we delete our copy.
